Degree Advisor

Know where you stand in your degree. Degree Advisor compares your transcript with your program's published requirements and shows what you have completed, what is in progress, and what remains.

How it works

  • Create an account and choose your degree program.
  • Upload your own unofficial transcript.
  • See your credits earned, credits in progress, and which requirements are satisfied.

Degree Advisor gives advice only. It never changes any academic record.

Email we send

All email comes from raghusrram.me and is transactional:

  • Account verification: one message with a link when you sign up.
  • Notifications you trigger: for example, when a result you asked for is ready.

We only email addresses that people enter themselves when they sign up. We send no marketing or newsletters, and we never buy, rent, or share email lists. Addresses that bounce or report a message as spam receive no further email.

Privacy

  • From an uploaded transcript we keep only the course rows: term, course, title, grade, and credits. We do not store your name, student ID, date of birth, address, or GPA from the file.
  • Your data is used only to show your own degree progress. It is never sold or shared.
